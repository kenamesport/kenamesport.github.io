THE STORY IN OUR NAME

BEHIND THE
NAME

KENAME (pronounced KEH-NAHM-EH) is inspired by the ancient Kushite Kingdoms of Kerma, Napata, and Meroe. These three civilizations in the Middle Nile Valley were ruled by queens, builders, and warriors.

In the spirit of Kush, KENAME represents legacy, leadership, and the enduring power of women to shape culture.
