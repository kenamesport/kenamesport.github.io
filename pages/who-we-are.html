KENAME Sport is built around the belief that women's football deserves experiences, visibility and opportunities that match the energy of its supporters.

OUR VISION

To help grow the women's game by creating meaningful ways for people to experience it. Through travel, matchday experiences, media and partnerships, KENAME connects supporters to the people, places and stories shaping women's football.
